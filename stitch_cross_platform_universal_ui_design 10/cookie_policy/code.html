What are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device by websites that you visit. They are widely used to make websites work, store user preferences, and provide analytics data to site owners.

2

Cookies We Deploy

A. Essential Cookies

Mandatory

Required to support merchant login sessions, CSRF security verification, and interface settings. Without these, the core application cannot function.

B. Functional Cookies

Optional

Remember your choices, such as language preference, theme mode (dark vs. light), and custom sidebar state settings.

C. Analytics & Performance

Anonymous

Track page visits and visitor flow to help us optimize speed and locate broken links. This includes our custom page enter/exit logging system.

3

How We Handle Consent

When first loading our public pages, a banner seeks consent for non-essential analytical cookies. Essential session cookies are loaded automatically to fulfill merchant functions.

4

Managing Your Cookies

Most browsers allow you to block or delete cookies through their preference panels. Note that blocking essential cookies will break login sessions and portal interfaces.
