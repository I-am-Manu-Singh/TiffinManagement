Merchant Subscription Cancellation

At TiffinManager (operated by ClungTech, Patna, Bihar, India), we prioritize flexibility. This Cancellation Policy outlines how you can manage, pause, or cancel your active merchant subscription at any time.

1

Your Right to Cancel

As a registered merchant on TiffinManager, you have full control over your account status. You can cancel your subscription at any time without termination fees, cancellation penalties, or hidden lock-in periods.
2

How to Process a Cancellation

2.1 Self-Service Cancellation Instant

Cancel your renewal directly through your dashboard in 4 steps:

  1. 1 Log in to your TiffinManager Vendor portal.
  2. 2 Navigate to Settings → Billing & Subscription.
  3. 3 Tap on "Cancel Auto-Renewal" or "Cancel Subscription".
  4. 4 Confirm cancellation to receive an automated email receipt confirming the request.
2.2 Support-Assisted Cancellation

If you face any issues or prefer our team to handle the request, email us at:

support@tiffinmanager.com

Include your Account ID. Processed within 24 business hours. Please submit at least 24 hours prior to renewal.

3

Cancellation Timing & Service Access

Access Retention When you cancel your subscription, automatic renewal is disabled. You will retain full access to all features (customer management, kitchen planning, driver dashboard) until the end of your current paid billing cycle.
No Prorated Credits Service remains active for the duration of the current paid period, and no partial or prorated credits are issued for the remainder of that billing cycle.
4

Free Trial Cancellations

If you are evaluating TiffinManager on a free trial, you can cancel at any point before the trial expires. Because we do not require a credit card for trial setup, your trial account will simply expire when the trial period runs out without any charges being applied.

5

Data Retention Post-Cancellation

We respect your business data and privacy when your subscription ends:

90-Day Safe Window

All merchant records, customer profiles, wallet logs, and delivery histories are securely archived for 90 days for fast reactivation.

Permanent Deletion

If not reactivated within 90 days, all data is permanently and irreversibly purged from our servers in compliance with data privacy standards.

6

Account Deletion vs. Cancellation

Canceling your subscription disables billing but preserves your configuration for easy future login. If you wish to permanently delete your account and erase all associated merchant data immediately (Right to Erasure), please send an email request to support@tiffinmanager.com from your registered owner email address.

7
