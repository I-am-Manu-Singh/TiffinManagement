# Tiffin Management Feature Map

Sources reviewed:
- https://www.tiffinmanager.com/
- https://mealawe.com/tiffin-service-in-kota/
- https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.mealawe.app&hl=en_IN

Implemented in the shared Compose Multiplatform app:
- Dashboard for scheduled meals, delivered counts, active customers, wallet collections, and activity feed.
- Customer profiles with plans, routes, balances, status, imports, preferences, vacation hold, and auto resume.
- Subscription plans for weekly, bi-weekly, monthly, trial, thali, bowl, and combo models.
- Customer ordering flow with thali selection, add-ons, start date, subscribe action, and exact drop-off location field.
- Menu customization for weekend delivery, extra chapati, homemade sweet, veg/non-veg preference, and weekly no-repeat meals.
- Home-food discovery for regional cuisines including Kashmiri, Punjabi, Rajasthani, Bengali, North Eastern, South Indian, Misal Pav, and Daal Baati Churma.
- Daily orders, bulk delivery, corporate/college group orders, catering service, party planning, no-minimum-order support, discounts, cashback, and daily deals.
- Kitchen planning for breakfast, lunch, and dinner time slots, cutoff windows, prep counts, menus, packaging notes, and locked counts.
- Delivery tracking with route sequence, addresses, delivered/skipped/on-hold/pending states, timestamps/proof placeholders, live ETA, map tracking, delivery partner location, and driver-friendly stops.
- Finance workspace with prepaid wallet, postpaid billing, debits, credits, skipped meal handling, invoices, reminders, ledger reasons, and payment modes for cash, credit/debit cards, net banking, PayTM, and UPI.
- Guided setup flow for adding customers, setting menus/cutoffs, tracking daily delivery, and billing monthly.
- Service trust features: homemade food, healthy daily meals, home kitchens, women home-chef empowerment, FSSAI-certified kitchens, eco-friendly biodegradable packaging, fast door-to-door/electric delivery, testimonials, support, privacy controls, data deletion request, and free trial messaging.

Platform coverage inherited from the CMP shell:
- Android app
- iOS app
- Desktop JVM app
- Browser JS app
- Browser Wasm app
