Why We Built This

Solving daily morning dispatch pressure

Tiffin service operations are operationally intense. Every morning starts with figuring out how many meals to cook, which customers paused, who changed quantity, and which route the driver should follow. Every month ends with billing reconciliation that can take days.

cancel

The Problem: WhatsApp groups, handwritten dairies, and Excel sheets make manual updates slip through during peak kitchen prep.

check_circle

Our Approach: Hard cutoff locks protect the cook stove, combined with real-time wallet ledgers that customers trust unconditionally.

What We Focus On

  • Simple screens that kitchen and delivery staff learn in 10 minutes
  • Transparent ledger wallet: verified balance with daily delivery audit
  • Strict cutoff rules protecting prep from late changes
  • Zero app installation required for staff or clients
