Customer Onboarding expand_more

Adding a new customer and creating their first subscription

Inline customer entry, dietary preferences (Veg/Non-Veg), custom spice notes, and route grouping.

Quickly enroll corporate desks, residential apartments, or hostel subscribers:

  • Go to Subscriptions > Add Customer or use the (+) floating button.
  • Input Phone Number (acts as their single sign-on customer portal key).
  • Select dietary tag (e.g., Pure Jain Veg or Halal Non-Veg).
  • Assign to a delivery route so the kitchen dispatch sheet groups stops sequentially.

How customers can pause/hold tiffin deliveries from their portal

Subscribers can schedule vacation pauses directly from the web link without needing manual WhatsApp calls.

Subscribers receive an SMS/WhatsApp link to their self-serve portal:

  • They select Pause Meal and pick calendar dates.
