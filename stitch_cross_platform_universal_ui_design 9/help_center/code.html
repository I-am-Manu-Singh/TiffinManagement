How to register and activate your Tiffin Center vendor account?

Step-by-step registration flow to create a vendor owner account, upload FSSAI compliance documents, and access your daily operational console.

1. Enter your mobile number to trigger instant SMS OTP verification.
2. Fill kitchen trade name and kitchen dispatch hub pincode.
3. Upload your 14-digit FSSAI food license number for auto-validation.
4. Add bank account details for direct prepaid subscription settlements.

Menu & Meal Plan Setup expand_more

Creating meal categories with delivery time windows and cutoff times

Configure categories (Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner) with precise delivery start/end slots and morning preparation cutoffs.

Navigate to Menu Settings > Delivery Slots. Specify your dispatch windows (e.g., Lunch: 12:00 PM - 1:30 PM). Set the customer change cutoff time (e.g., 9:00 AM) so orders lock automatically before ingredient prep begins.

Customer & Subscriptions expand_more

Adding a new customer and creating their first active subscription

Create customer subscriptions with inline contact onboarding, dietary tags, recurring weekly schedules, and initial wallet deposits.

Use the + New Customer quick button from Subscriptions screen. Select veg/non-veg dietary tags, pick Mon-Fri or Mon-Sat cycle, and enter their starting prepaid balance to automatically issue daily meal tokens.

Wallet & Billing expand_more

How the prepaid customer wallet billing engine works

Automated wallet deductions at category cutoff times with tamper-proof audit ledgers and negative balance alerts.

The billing engine processes locks daily at the cutoff hour. Meals are deducted from the subscriber's balance. If a wallet drops below threshold, automated WhatsApp balance reminders are triggered.

Hold Rules & Pausing expand_more

How customers pause tiffin deliveries from their own portal

Subscribers can self-service vacation holds without calling the kitchen, preserving meal credits automatically.

Subscribers log in with OTP, select Pause Meals, pick a date range, and confirm before cutoff. The Kitchen console immediately reduces portion prep counts for those dates.

View aggregated preparation quantities for rotis, sabzis, dal liters, and dietary adjustments in one consolidated view.
