2. Dispatch & Label Printing
expand_more

Customer and meal metadata is processed exclusively to deliver warm, correct food packets on schedule:

receipt_long 58mm Thermal Kitchen Stickers

Customer names, lunchbox meal types (e.g. Standard Veg x1), and dietary tags are parsed to connected Bluetooth/ESC-POS thermal printers for physical lunchbox stickering. We do not print sensitive payment records on outer containers.

chat WhatsApp Menu & Emergency SMS

Customer phone numbers are utilized solely to broadcast daily kitchen rotations (sabzi / roti changes) or critical delay alerts via WhatsApp Cloud Business APIs. No telemarketing lists are rented or sold.
