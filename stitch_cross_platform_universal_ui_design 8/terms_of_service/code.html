Termination & Retention

Users may cancel active subscriptions at any time through the console. Following settlement of all outstanding negative wallet ledgers, remaining balances will be refunded to the source account within 3 to 5 business banking days.

Order history, delivery addresses, and GST billing documentation are retained securely for up to 180 days in compliance with Indian financial archiving standards.
